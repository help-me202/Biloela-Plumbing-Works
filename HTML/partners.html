Biloela Hydroexcavation

(Non Destructive Digging)

Hydro Excavation is an effective and safe alternative to other methods of excavation for a wide range of uses; pot holing, locating water leaks, sewer breaks, trenching under paths, etc. It's a process that minimises damage to infrastructure, underground services and other surrounds with efficient clean-up once finished. Safety is our priority and our trucks meet stringent safety standards. With features like remote-controlled operation, pressure relief systems and advanced filtration mechanisms, they minimise risks associated with waste handling and disposal. Our experienced operators ensure that every job is executed with precision and adherence to safety regulations.
