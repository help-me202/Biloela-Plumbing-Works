Hook Bins/Skip Bins

  • Scrap metal bins are provided free of charge
  • General waste bins
  • Hook bins 8 - 20 cubic metres
  • Skip bins 3 - 6 cubic metres

Items NOT ALLOWED in our general waste bins include:

  • No oils or liquids
  • No batteries
  • No tyres

Bins can be filled to the top rim of the bin as any excess or over filling of the bin may result in additional fees.
