LPG Exchange & Delivery Network

Hassle-free, safe, local, reliable LP Gas bottle exchange and delivery to your doorstep. How home LP Gas bottle exchange works: The typical home LPG exchange service involves two 45kg cylinders connected by a changeover valve and fitted with gas regulators. When one cylinder is empty, you simply turn the changeover valve so it points to the full cylinder – this is your reminder to order a fresh 45kg LPG cylinder. Our delivery driver will safely exchange the empty cylinder with a full cylinder.

Swap'n'Go

As the name suggests, our SWAP'n’GO program is all about swapping over your empty gas cylinder with a full one and off you go! No waiting around for someone to manually refill your gas cylinder. You can swap over your empty 8.5kg or 3.7kg gas cylinder for a full one.
